🌸 Pink Noise Reference

-12dB
Step 1: Play Pink Noise at -12dBFS. Bring ALL faders to -∞.
Step 2: Raise each track until you can barely hear it over the noise. This is its correct level.
Step 3: The Kick & Bass should be louder (you should hear them), the rest near equal or slightly below.
Why it works: Pink noise has equal energy per octave — same as human hearing. Your mix will be spectrally balanced.

📊 Spectrum Reference

Sub Bass (20-60Hz): Kick transient, Bass fundamental. Cut everything else here.
Bass (60-250Hz): Bass body. Max 1 element dominant.
Low-Mid (250-500Hz): Mud zone. Cut pads & leads here to clean the mix.
Mid (500-2kHz): Presence, attack. Leads & vocals live here.
High-Mid (2-6kHz): Psychedelic frequencies. Autofilter sweep target.
Air (6-20kHz): Shimmer. Boost pads +1-2dB at 8kHz for openness.
